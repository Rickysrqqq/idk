## Tips for Grok
- Most video generators cap each clip at around 6–15 seconds. Generate the master prompt + 2–3 scenes at a time, then join the clips in an editor.
- Generators tend to garble small text. Generate the visuals without text and add the headlines, pills and logo in an editor (or tell it to leave those areas clean).
- Keep your reference photos attached to every generation so the parts don't get redesigned.
